    <h3 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-8 mb-4">Benefits of Using a Home Dehumidifier</h3>
    <p class="mb-4">Installing a dehumidifier offers several long-term advantages for your home and family.</p>

    <h4 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-6 mb-2">Healthier Indoor Air</h4>
    <p class="mb-4">High humidity encourages the growth of mold spores, bacteria, and dust mites. By controlling moisture, a dehumidifier creates a cleaner indoor environment that is more comfortable for everyone.</p>

    <h4 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-6 mb-2">Protects Your Home</h4>
    <p class="mb-4">Excess moisture can damage furniture, books, electronics, mattresses, carpets, and wooden flooring. A dehumidifier helps extend the life of these valuable household items.</p>

    <h4 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-6 mb-2">Eliminates Musty Odors</h4>
    <p class="mb-4">Rooms with high humidity often develop unpleasant smells caused by mold and mildew. Maintaining balanced humidity keeps your home smelling fresh.</p>

    <h4 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-6 mb-2">Better Sleep and Comfort</h4>
    <p class="mb-4">Lower humidity levels make indoor spaces feel cooler and more comfortable, especially during India's humid summer and monsoon seasons.</p>

    <h3 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-8 mb-4">How to Choose the Best Dehumidifier for Your Home</h3>
    <p class="mb-4">Choosing the right dehumidifier depends on several important factors.</p>

    <h4 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-6 mb-2">Room Size</h4>
    <p class="mb-4">Select a dehumidifier with the appropriate capacity for the room where it will be used. Larger spaces require higher moisture removal capacity.</p>

    <h4 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-6 mb-2">Daily Moisture Removal Capacity</h4>
    <p class="mb-4">The capacity is usually measured in litres per day (LPD). A higher LPD rating means the unit can remove more moisture within 24 hours.</p>

    <h4 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-6 mb-2">Water Tank Capacity</h4>
    <p class="mb-4">A larger water tank allows the unit to operate for longer before it needs to be emptied.</p>

    <h4 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-6 mb-2">Continuous Drainage</h4>
    <p class="mb-4">If you plan to use the dehumidifier for extended periods, choose a model that supports continuous drainage through a hose connection.</p>

    <h4 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-6 mb-2">Noise Level</h4>
    <p class="mb-4">Bedrooms, study rooms, and offices benefit from quieter dehumidifiers that operate with minimal sound.</p>

    <h4 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-6 mb-2">Smart Features</h4>
    <p class="mb-4">Modern dehumidifiers often include a digital humidity display, auto humidity control, timer function, child lock, auto restart after power failure, automatic defrost, and full water tank indicator.</p>

    <h3 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-8 mb-4">Home Dehumidifier vs Air Purifier</h3>
    <p class="mb-4">Although they improve indoor comfort, a dehumidifier and an <a href="/air-purifiers" class="bg-[linear-gradient(currentColor,currentColor)] bg-[length:100%_1px] bg-no-repeat bg-[position:100%_100%] transition-[background-size] duration-300 hover:bg-[length:0%_1px]">air purifier</a> serve different purposes:</p>
    <ul class="list-none pl-6 my-6 space-y-2.5">
      <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">A <strong>dehumidifier</strong> removes excess moisture from the air, helping prevent mold, mildew, and dampness.</li>
      <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">An <strong>air purifier</strong> removes airborne particles such as dust, pollen, smoke, pet dander, and allergens.</li>
    </ul>
    <p class="mb-4">Many modern units combine both functions, providing humidity control along with advanced air filtration for a healthier indoor environment.</p>

    <h3 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-8 mb-4">Tips for Using a Home Dehumidifier Efficiently</h3>
    <ul class="list-none pl-6 my-6 space-y-2.5">
      <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Keep doors and windows closed while the unit is operating.</li>
      <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Clean the air filter regularly.</li>
      <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Empty the water tank when it becomes full.</li>
      <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Place the unit away from walls to ensure proper airflow.</li>
      <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Maintain indoor humidity between 40% and 60%.</li>
      <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Service the dehumidifier periodically for optimal performance.</li>
    </ul>
