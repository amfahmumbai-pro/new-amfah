      <p class="lead text-lg text-brand-gray-dark font-medium mb-6">
        A corrugated box may look simple, but it plays an important role in protecting products throughout the supply chain. Whether it's pharmaceuticals, electronics, food products, cosmetics, or e-commerce shipments, the carton is the first layer of protection.
      </p>

      <h3 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-8 mb-4">Strong Packaging Starts with the Right Storage Conditions</h3>
      <p class="mb-4">However, even the highest-quality corrugated box can lose its strength if it's stored in a humid environment.</p>
      <p class="mb-4">Many warehouses focus on organizing inventory and maximizing storage space but overlook one invisible factor that can quietly damage thousands of cartons—humidity.</p>

      <h3 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-8 mb-4">Moisture Is One of the Biggest Enemies of Corrugated Boxes</h3>
      <p class="mb-4">Corrugated boxes are made from paper-based materials that naturally absorb moisture from the surrounding air.</p>
      <p class="mb-4">During the monsoon season or in coastal regions, humidity levels often remain high for long periods.</p>
      <p class="mb-4">As cartons absorb moisture, the paper fibers begin to soften. The change may not be visible immediately, but over time the box becomes weaker and less reliable.</p>
      <p class="mb-4">By the time the problem is noticed, large quantities of packaging may already be affected.</p>

      <h3 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-8 mb-4">What Happens When Boxes Absorb Moisture?</h3>
      <p class="mb-4">Humidity doesn't destroy cartons overnight. The damage develops gradually. Common problems include:</p>
      <ul class="list-disc pl-6 my-6 space-y-2.5">
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Soft cartons</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Warped box panels</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Reduced stacking strength</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Bent corners</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Crushed boxes</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Torn edges</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Poor printing quality</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Loose adhesive joints</li>
      </ul>
      <p class="mb-4">As the structure weakens, the box can no longer provide the protection it was designed for.</p>

      <h3 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-8 mb-4">The Product Inside Is Also at Risk</h3>
      <p class="mb-4">A damaged carton doesn't just affect packaging. It also increases the risk of product damage during storage and transportation.</p>
      <p class="mb-4">Weak cartons may collapse while stacked, allowing products inside to shift, break, or become scratched.</p>
      <p class="mb-4">Even when the product remains safe, damaged packaging creates a poor impression and may lead customers to question its quality.</p>

      <h3 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-8 mb-4">Warehouses Experience Constant Changes in Humidity</h3>
      <p class="mb-4">Humidity inside a warehouse changes throughout the day. Some common reasons include:</p>
      <ul class="list-disc pl-6 my-6 space-y-2.5">
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Loading dock doors opening frequently</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Rainy weather</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Coastal climates</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Poor air circulation</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Condensation</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Temperature fluctuations</li>
      </ul>
      <p class="mb-4">These conditions allow moisture to spread across the storage area, affecting cartons stored on racks and pallets.</p>

      <h3 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-8 mb-4">Long-Term Storage Requires Better Humidity Management</h3>
      <p class="mb-4">Many businesses store packaging materials for weeks or even months before they are used. During this time, cartons remain exposed to the surrounding environment every day.</p>
      <p class="mb-4">The longer they stay in humid conditions, the greater the chance of moisture absorption. This is especially important for:</p>
      <ul class="list-disc pl-6 my-6 space-y-2.5">
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Export packaging</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Pharmaceutical cartons</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">FMCG packaging</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Printed cartons</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">E-commerce shipping boxes</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Spare parts packaging</li>
      </ul>
      <p class="mb-4">Maintaining stable humidity helps preserve packaging quality throughout storage.</p>

      <h3 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-8 mb-4">Simple Storage Practices Make a Big Difference</h3>
      <p class="mb-4">Good warehouse management reduces the chances of moisture damage.</p>

      <h4 class="text-lg font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-6 mb-2">Keep Cartons Off the Floor</h4>
      <p class="mb-4">Always store corrugated boxes on pallets or storage racks. This prevents direct contact with floor moisture and improves airflow underneath the cartons.</p>

      <h4 class="text-lg font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-6 mb-2">Leave Space Around Storage Racks</h4>
      <p class="mb-4">Avoid placing cartons tightly against walls. Proper spacing allows air to circulate freely and reduces moisture buildup.</p>

      <h4 class="text-lg font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-6 mb-2">Rotate Inventory Regularly</h4>
      <p class="mb-4">Using the First-In, First-Out (FIFO) method prevents cartons from remaining in storage longer than necessary, reducing prolonged exposure to humid conditions.</p>

      <h4 class="text-lg font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-6 mb-2">Inspect Packaging Frequently</h4>
      <p class="mb-4">Regular inspections make it easier to identify early signs of moisture damage. Check for:</p>
      <ul class="list-disc pl-6 my-6 space-y-2.5">
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Soft corners</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Bulging cartons</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Water stains</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Mold spots</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Damaged labels</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Collapsed stacks</li>
      </ul>
      <p class="mb-4">Addressing these issues early prevents larger inventory losses.</p>

      <h3 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-8 mb-4">Is Air Conditioning Enough?</h3>
      <p class="mb-4">Air conditioning can make a warehouse feel cooler, but cooling the air isn't the same as controlling humidity.</p>
      <p class="mb-4">During humid weather, moisture levels may remain high even when the temperature is comfortable.</p>
      <p class="mb-4">That's why warehouses storing valuable packaging materials often rely on dedicated humidity control systems instead of depending solely on air conditioning.</p>

      <h3 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-8 mb-4">Benefits of Maintaining the Right Humidity</h3>
      <p class="mb-4">Keeping humidity under control helps businesses protect both packaging materials and finished products. Some of the biggest advantages include:</p>
      <ul class="list-disc pl-6 my-6 space-y-2.5">
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Stronger corrugated boxes</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Better stacking performance</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Fewer damaged shipments</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Cleaner packaging</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Reduced product losses</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Longer storage life</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Lower replacement costs</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Better customer satisfaction</li>
      </ul>
      <p class="mb-4">Instead of replacing damaged cartons, businesses can prevent the problem before it starts.</p>

      <h3 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-8 mb-4">Industries That Depend on Corrugated Box Storage</h3>
      <p class="mb-4">Humidity control is valuable across many industries, including:</p>
      <ul class="list-disc pl-6 my-6 space-y-2.5">
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Pharmaceutical manufacturing</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Food processing</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">FMCG</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Electronics</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Automotive</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Cosmetics</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Printing and packaging</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">E-commerce</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Warehousing</li>
        <li class="leading-relaxed text-brand-gray-dark/90">Logistics</li>
      </ul>
      <p class="mb-4">Every industry that relies on corrugated packaging benefits from keeping storage conditions stable.</p>

      <h3 class="text-xl font-bold font-display text-brand-navy mt-8 mb-4">Protect Packaging Before It Leaves the Warehouse</h3>
      <p class="mb-4">Corrugated boxes are designed to protect products, but they also need protection while they are in storage.</p>
      <p class="mb-4">Maintaining the right humidity helps cartons remain strong, improves warehouse efficiency, reduces packaging waste, and supports safer transportation.</p>
